      VRIKSH Mobile Application – User & Operations Manual

      EUDR Traceability • Geolocation • Tree Inventory • Booking & Lot Management
      Version 1.0 | 30 October 2026

      Document information

      Purpose. A practical, step-by-step guide to the mobile app workflows documented in the supplied presentation.

      Intended users. Farmers, non-farmer supply-chain participants, timber traders and operational support teams.

      Source basis. VRIKSH-APP-GUIDE.pdf. App-specific details are kept where visible; missing technical or policy details are identified rather than assumed.

      ImportantThis manual explains app use. It is not a legal opinion or a guarantee of EUDR compliance.

      1. About this manual

      This manual explains the user-facing workflows shown in the supplied VRIKSH Mobile App presentation. It is for farmers, timber traders and other non-farmer participants, and for the operational teams supporting onboarding and traceability.

      The presentation describes VRIKSH as a mobile solution for collecting geolocation information and supporting compliance processes associated with the European Union Deforestation Regulation (EUDR). This manual follows its features and sequence.

      2. Purpose and objectives of the VRIKSH App

      The app connects participants in the tree and timber supply chain and supports traceability through location-based listings and transaction workflows. Key objectives:

      • Connect farmers and buyers through a marketplace.
      • Support supply-chain tracking.
      • Collect and collate geolocation information.
      • Facilitate trade among farmers, saw millers, timber traders, manufacturers and merchants.

      A Web Admin Portal manages user profiles, approves batch codes and monitors transactions.

      3. User categories and access

      • Farmer. Farm creation, tree addition and tree inventory are farmer-only functions.
      • Non-Farmer. A separate registration flow serves timber traders and other supply-chain participants.
      • Web administrator. Oversees user profiles, batch-code approvals and transaction monitoring. Detailed administrator screens are not in the presentation.

      4. Registration and account creation

      4.1 Farmer registration

      1. Open the app and choose the account creation / sign-up route.
      2. Select the farmer registration path.
      3. Enter the requested farmer and contact details.
      4. Complete the email verification step: check the email account used and follow the app’s instructions.
      5. Complete the remaining account fields and submit.
      6. Return to the login screen and sign in with your registered credentials.

      4.2 Non-Farmer registration

      1. Select Create Account for non-farmers / other users.
      2. Complete the business or participant information, including contact and business details.
      3. Upload the supporting business documents the screen requests, where applicable.
      4. Complete the remaining account and password fields and submit.
      5. Sign in once any verification or approval requirement shown by the app is complete.
      ImportantThe guide shows screenshots of the forms but gives no field-by-field list, document checklist, password policy or approval service standard. Follow the live form labels and instructions.

      5. Login, home page and language

      1. Open the app and enter your registered login credentials.
      2. Use the sign-in action to access the account.
      3. The farmer home page links to the farm and tree-related functions.
      4. A language control offers English and Hindi.
      5. Review the home page and navigation before starting a new farm or tree record.
      Keep credentials privateDo not share passwords or verification codes. If login fails, recheck your email/username, password, network connection and any on-screen error.

      6. Adding a farm (Farmer only)

      The Add New Farm workflow has a farm information form and a map-based location and boundary step.

      1. Sign in with a farmer account.
      2. Open My Farms and choose to add a new farm.
      3. Enter the requested farm information. Check spelling before proceeding.
      4. Use the map / geolocation interface to identify the farm location.
      5. Mark the farm boundary by creating the polygon, following the actual boundary as closely as possible.
      6. Review the mapped area and details. Correct misplaced boundary points before saving.
      7. Save the farm record and check that it appears in My Farms.
      Good practiceCapture the boundary on or near the site; confirm location permission and device location services are on; avoid including neighbouring land, roads or unrelated plots. These are operational recommendations, not extra app features.

      7. Adding trees (Farmer only)

      The Add Trees flow is farmer-only. The guide states that 44 tree species have been added to the app.

      1. Open the relevant farm or the My Trees / Add Trees area.
      2. Start the Add Tree process.
      3. Select the tree species from the list (a catalogue of 44).
      4. Enter the tree details the form requests.
      5. Add the location or supporting information the app prompts for.
      6. Review and save the tree record.
      7. Repeat for additional trees or groups as needed.
      Choose species carefullyDo not guess when identification is uncertain; ask the field expert or operations team. The presentation does not list all 44 species or define every tree data field.

      8. Tree inventory and batch records

      Tree Inventory is a farmer-only function. Records appear in a list with batch-related information and record actions.

      1. Open My Trees / Tree Inventory from the farmer account.
      2. Review the records and their farm or batch information.
      3. Use any list or filter controls to find the record.
      4. Open a record to review its details.
      5. Use record actions only when required and authorised. Deletion has a confirmation step, so treat it as consequential.
      6. Confirm that any permitted update is reflected in the inventory.

      Not defined in the presentation: batch-code format, approval rules, inventory export, audit history. Batch-code approval is a Web Admin Portal responsibility.

      9. Booking workflow: farmer and timber trader

      The Booking section has separate Farmer and Timber Trader perspectives, supporting interaction between the two.

      1. Sign in with the appropriate participant account.
      2. Open the Booking area.
      3. Review the booking details for the relevant party or transaction.
      4. Enter the details requested. Confirm the farmer, trader, tree / lot information and quantities match the intended transaction.
      5. Submit the booking or booking response.
      6. Review the status or confirmation shown.
      Before submittingVerify the parties, product / lot reference, quantity and displayed commercial or logistical details. Payment, contract terms, delivery scheduling and cancellation rules are not specified and should follow your business process.

      10. Creating lots / grouping

      The Create Lots / Grouping workflow has screens for creating a group and viewing My Inventory.

      1. Open the lot / group creation function from the inventory or workflow area.
      2. Enter the group / lot information requested.
      3. Select the eligible tree or inventory records to include.
      4. Review the selected records and group details.
      5. Save the lot or group.
      6. Check the lot in the inventory or group listing and confirm the intended records are included.
      Do not combine unrelated or unverified recordsKeep a clear link between each lot and its underlying farm and tree records. Lot eligibility rules, maximum size and identifier format are not defined.

      11. Processing and booking request

      The final workflow, Processing & Booking Request, moves between My Inventory and processing / booking views.

      1. Open the relevant inventory or lot record.
      2. Start the processing / booking request using the action on screen.
      3. Complete the information requested in the form.
      4. Confirm the request matches the correct inventory / lot and intended transaction.
      5. Submit using the app’s submit / confirm action.
      6. Return to the inventory or request view and check the status.

      Processing stages, status meanings, approval roles and service-level timelines are not defined. Rely on the live app’s status labels and your organisation’s approved procedure.

      12. Documents and EUDR traceability

      Uploading legal and supply-chain documents is a key feature, alongside geolocation integration, polygon creation, batch tree ID creation and automated data conversion.

      Upload correct, legible and current documents for the relevant user, farm, tree, lot or transaction, and check each belongs to the selected record.

      Keep the chain consistent: participant identity, farm location, mapped boundary, tree / species record, inventory / batch, lot grouping and booking / processing request. A mismatch at one stage can weaken the traceability record.

      Not legal adviceThis manual does not interpret EUDR legal requirements or certify that using the app alone establishes compliance. Follow your organisation’s approved EUDR due-diligence procedure.

      13. Web Admin Portal – overview

      The Web Admin Portal oversees platform operations: user-profile management, batch-code approval and transaction monitoring.

      Administrators should perform only the functions assigned to their role and follow internal approval controls. Verify identity and supporting information before changing profiles or approving batch codes. Watch transactions for missing or inconsistent information and refer exceptions to the responsible owner.

      Portal navigation, role permissions, audit logs, user suspension, approval criteria and reporting are not shown and should be documented in a separate administrator SOP.

      14. Data quality, privacy and security practices

      • Enter accurate participant and contact information.
      • Capture the correct farm location and boundary; review the polygon before saving.
      • Choose the correct species and avoid unsupported assumptions.
      • Link tree records to the correct farm and lot.
      • Upload clear, relevant documents to the correct record.
      • Review booking and processing details before submission.
      • Keep login credentials confidential and use only authorised accounts.
      • Do not create duplicate records to work around an error; contact support or operations.
      • Report incorrect geolocation, duplicate or missing records and unexpected status changes promptly.

      These are recommended operational controls for reliable use of the workflows described in the source guide.

      15. Basic troubleshooting

      Unable to log inConfirm the registered identifier and password, check your connection, and use the app’s password recovery or support route if shown.
      Email verification not receivedCheck the registered address, spam / junk folder and network. Request another message only if the app offers it.
      Map or location not workingEnable device location services and app permission, check connectivity and retry from the correct site. If the boundary is still inaccurate, do not submit an unreliable polygon.
      Farm / tree record not visibleRefresh or reopen the list, confirm the correct account and farm are selected, and check for a submission or approval status.
      Document upload failsConfirm the file type and size follow the on-screen instructions, check connectivity and retry. Supported formats and size limits are not specified.
      Booking / request status unclearOpen the record and review the status. If still unclear, share the record reference and a screenshot with support.
      Escalation details to provideUser role, registered account identifier (never your password), affected farm / tree / lot / booking reference, date and time, device details, exact error message and screenshot.

      16. Operational checklist

      • Before field data entry: confirm the correct farmer account, farm / site and device location settings.
      • Before saving a farm: verify farm details, map position and polygon boundary.
      • Before saving a tree: verify species, farm association and all required fields.
      • Before creating a lot: verify the selected inventory records and their traceability links.
      • Before submitting a booking / request: verify counterparties, lot / inventory reference, quantities and all displayed details.
      • After submission: confirm the record appears in the relevant list and note any reference or status shown.

      17. Scope and limitations of this manual

      This manual is based on the supplied 15-page VRIKSH Mobile App presentation. It expands the pictured workflows into practical instructions but does not replace the live app, formal training, legal advice or an approved internal SOP.

      Where the presentation does not specify field names, button labels, file formats, approval rules, status definitions or service timelines, this manual avoids inventing them. Screen layouts and functions may change as the app is updated. Screenshots from the source presentation are in the original PDF.
