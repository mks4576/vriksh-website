Welcome to VRIKSH.

The EPCH VRIKSH timber legality assessment and verification standard is for businesses that want to track their wood products accurately, and make credible claims about legal origin and transport.

Know moreOur history
  • Legal right to harvest and tradeEach supply chain is checked for lawful origin.
